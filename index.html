About

Where healthcare ops meets analytics

I started on the operations side of healthcare revenue cycle management — posting insurance payments, reconciling claims, chasing down denials. That's where I learned that most "data problems" are really process problems wearing a spreadsheet.

Now I build the dashboards I used to wish I'd had: Power BI reports that surface denial patterns, payer performance and collection trends before they become a backlog. I'm equally comfortable in SQL, Python (Pandas/NumPy) and Excel, and I care about the story a dataset is trying to tell.
